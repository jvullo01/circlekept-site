A private family chat and location sharing app. End-to-end encrypted. Data Not Collected.

Home · Support · Privacy

About

Circlekept is a big-text, grandparent-friendly family group chat for iPhone, joined by family invite only, with opt-in location sharing and arrival alerts. It's coming soon.

Support

Questions or problems? Email jvappsupport+circlekept@proton.me.

Privacy

Circlekept collects no data. There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no crash-reporting tools.

Messages and locations are end-to-end encrypted on your iPhone and kept in your family's own iCloud shared space. Only the family members you approve can read them. The developer can't read them, and neither can Apple.

Location sharing is opt-in for each person and can be paused or turned off at any time.
